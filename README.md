# RhinE — An Audio Archive

*A foobar2000 theme.*

**[Installation](INSTALL.md)** · [Guide](GUIDE.md) · [Changelog](CHANGELOG.md)

**[安装指南](安装指南.md)** · [其他信息](其他信息.md) · [更新日志](CHANGELOG.md)

An archive terminal for listening: Swiss typography, a monochrome palette with one accent colour, and a sci-fi layer.
Your albums stand as rendered specimen cases on shelves, a Möbius signal ring pulses with the music, and synced lyrics
are fetched online when your files have none.

## Features

- **Archive:** every album, or every track, as a rendered case on shelves, or a cover grid; inspect, play, rate and keep favourites.
- **Artist files:** who made the album, from MusicBrainz, Wikipedia and ListenBrainz: picture, biography, genres and related
  artists, on the album file or in a file of its own with a map of related artists.
- **Playlists:** a playlist manager, a track rail, the native playlist in two presets and a profile card with the queue.
- **Lyrics:** synced lyrics from your files, LRCLIB or NetEase Cloud Music, with an optional translation and romanization.
- **Signal:** a rendered ∞ ring, a spectrum and the whole-track waveform, or a classic player screen.
- **Style:** eleven case skins and eight colour schemes in light and dark, your own case and scheme, a wallpaper,
  title tags, three fonts.
- English, Simplified and Traditional Chinese, Japanese, Korean, French and German. The theme updates itself.

## Requirements

Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit), foobar2000 v2 (64-bit), [Columns UI](https://github.com/reupen/columns_ui/releases) and
[JSplitter](https://github.com/dima-lur/jsplitter/releases). The installer adds the two components when they are missing.

## Install

Download the portable bundle from [Releases](https://github.com/ericzhang12111-cell/RhinE/releases) and double-click
`Setup 一键安装.cmd`, or download the theme and double-click `install.cmd`. Step by step: **[INSTALL.md](INSTALL.md)**.

Keys, settings, how each view works, troubleshooting and known limits: **[GUIDE.md](GUIDE.md)**.
